Hey Signal users! Are you feeling the heat from recent hack attempts like you’re starring in a cyber-thriller? Well, you’re not alone. Hackers from Iran and post-Soviet countries have been trying to sneak into Signal accounts like it's Black Friday at a tech store. But don’t worry, we've got the scoop on how to protect your digital fortress!

### Hackers Be Gone: The Two-Factor Tango

First things first, Signal is updating its Terms of Service and Privacy Policy to crank up the security dial to eleven. They're introducing Mandatory Two-factor Verification. Think of it as your bouncer at the digital club door. No two-factor, no entry!

### Backup Your Chats or Regret It Forever

Signal's also reminding us that if we don't back up our messages and media, they might disappear faster than cookies at a bake sale. Follow these steps to secure your chats:

1. **Settings**: Tap your way into the Signal treasure chest.
2. **Backups**: Find it, love it, enable it.
3. **View Recovery Key**: This key is your digital life raft. Copy it!
4. **Next, Next, Continue**: Channel your inner tech wizard.
5. **Choose Your Backup Plan**: Pick a plan that suits your style—like picking the perfect emoji.

### Beware of the Phish - It's Not Just for Sushi Lovers!

Phishing attacks are like the annoying telemarketers of the cyber world. If you or someone you know falls victim to one of these pesky scams, report it faster than you can say “cybersecurity.” 

- **File a complaint** with IC3.
- **Report it** to your local FBI field office.
- **Contact CISA** through their Incident Reporting System or call 1-844-Say-CISA.

For a deep dive into spoofing, phishing, and everything in between, check out the FBI's guidance. It's like the superhero handbook for internet safety.

### Stay Updated and Stay Awesome

Finally, keep your eyes peeled for any pop-ups from Signal. Accept updates like you’re accepting an award for Best Cyber Safe User of the Year. And remember, if you ever need help, just send a little "/help" message, and Signal’s got your back.

Stay safe, stay funny, and keep those hackers at bay!
