Picture this: You're on a quest to find a reputable substance use disorder clinic online. You've got the clinic's name typed into the search bar and you're ready to get the help you need. But wait! Little did you know, lurking in the shadows of the internet, are some dodgy businesses ready to pounce like digital ninjas. Welcome to the wacky world of search result impersonation scams!

In a recent case against Evoke Wellness, LLC, the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) uncovered a sneaky scheme where scammers paid for their ads to appear as top search results. These ads cunningly mimicked the clinics people were actually searching for, complete with the clinic’s name, but with a twist—an entirely different website link and phone number. Yikes!

Imagine you’re searching for your local clinic, and the top result looks like the real deal. You click, you call, and—bam! You've just been duped into contacting the impersonator. Cue the telemarketer with a generic greeting like “Admissions” or “Recovery helpline,” skillfully dodging any direct questions about whether you’ve reached the right clinic. It’s like being stuck in a bad episode of a detective show where the villain is always one step ahead.

So, how do you avoid becoming the unsuspecting star of this unfortunate drama? Here are some tips to keep those scammers at bay:

1. **Scroll, scroll, scroll!** Don’t just click on the first result you see. Paid ads often appear at the top, and some might be from impersonators. Scroll down to find the actual clinic’s official website.

2. **Direct is best!** If you know the clinic’s website address, type it directly into your browser’s address bar. This ensures you’re on the right site, avoiding any impersonator shenanigans.

3. **Double-check contact info!** Verify the phone number and email on the clinic’s official website. A little extra typing could save you a lot of hassle.

4. **Spot the shady business? Speak up!** If you stumble upon any suspicious business practices, don’t just fume silently—report it! Visit [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://ReportFraud.ftc.gov) and let the FTC know.

Remember, when it comes to finding help, don’t let the impersonators get the best of you. With a little vigilance and some smart searching, you can outsmart those sneaky scammers and get connected to the real help you need.

Feel free to share this post and help spread awareness! After all, we all deserve to find the right help without jumping through hoops or dodging digital tricksters.

For more details on this case, check out the original article: [FTC v. Evoke Wellness, LLC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/press-releases/2023/10/evoke-wellness-llc-settles-ftc-charges-impersonating-competitors-search-ads).
