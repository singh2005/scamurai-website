Ah, the last lazy days of summer—the perfect time to kick back, relax, and... teach your kids about internet safety? Absolutely! Before you roll your eyes, let me introduce you to Youville, an imaginary town brought to life by the FTC where learning online safety is as fun as a day at the beach. So grab your 8-12-year-olds and let's dive into the viral vortex of Youville!

**Welcome to Youville!**  
Youville is a quirky town where Youbies live and thrive—until the mischievous Claud shows up to cause chaos. As your kids help the Youbies fend off Claud’s antics, they'll tackle activities designed to teach them about online safety. It’s like being a superhero, but instead of capes, we're talking about bookmarks and activity sheets!

**What’s in Store?**  
In Youville, your kids will learn essential skills like:

- **Protecting Personal Information:** No, kids, sharing your home address with a stranger online is not the same as swapping Pokémon cards.
- **Smart Posting Practices:** Think twice before posting that goofy picture of your brother. Future employers might not appreciate his spaghetti hairdo.
- **Scam Spotting:** If it sounds too good to be true, it probably is. Spoiler alert: that Nigerian prince still doesn’t need your help.
- **Handling Online Bullying:** Because even Youbies know that online trolls belong under the bridge, not in your chat.

**Activities to Engage and Educate**  
Does your kid love puzzles, mini-adventures, coloring, cutting, or gluing? Great! They can do all that with Youville’s free materials. Just head over to [ftc.gov/Youville](ftc.gov/Youville) to download and print the activity sheets. You can work through them during downtime at home or while traveling—because who said road trips can’t be educational?

**Plan Your Safety Sessions**  
Youville comes with lesson plans that include quick, 10-minute mini-lessons. These come equipped with conversation starters to get your kids talking about their digital world. It’s like a TED Talk, but with crayons and glue sticks!

**Craft Your Own JumboFan**  
Have your kids cut out blades and attach them to their very own JumboFan. What's a JumboFan, you ask? Well, you’ll have to check out Youville to find out—no spoilers here!

**Get Your Free Swag!**  
Order free Youville bookmarks to keep your kids’ summer reading on track. They’re also perfect for the local library, so spread the safety love!

So there you have it, folks. With Youville, you’re not just teaching your kids how to navigate the digital landscape—you’re preparing them to be the cyber-savvy superheroes they’re meant to be. Go ahead, visit [ftc.gov/Youville](ftc.gov/Youville) and start your adventure today!
