Welcome to the wild world of elections, where your vote is like a golden ticket to democracy. But beware, because lurking in the shadows are sneaky folks trying to pull off federal election crimes. Fear not, dear voter! We’ve got the lowdown on these electoral antics and how you can keep your vote safe from the shenanigans.

**Election Crimes: The Rogue's Gallery**

Think of these as the greatest hits of election mischief:

- **False Info Fiends:** Pretending to be someone else when registering to vote? Not cool.
- **Vote Multipliers:** Voting more than once might sound like a superpower, but it’s actually a crime.
- **Ballot Bandits:** Changing ballot markings or tampering with ballots is a big no-no.
- **Vote Vendors:** Selling your vote is not a buy-one-get-one-free deal.
- **Threat Titans:** Bullying voters with physical or financial harm? That’s a villain move.
- **Liar, Liar, Pants on Fire:** Spreading lies about when, where, or how to vote is seriously uncool.
- **Fundraising Fiascos:** Federal employees, sorry, no political fundraising for you.
- **Contribution Chaos:** Over-the-limit donations and sneaky straw donor schemes are off-limits.
- **Foreign Fiascos:** Contributions from foreign lands are strictly forbidden.
- **Cash Crooks:** Using campaign funds for a personal shopping spree? That’s a scandal waiting to happen.
- **Non-citizen Votes:** Only citizens get to partake in this democratic dance.

**How to Keep Your Vote Crime-Free**

- **Plan Your Vote Party:** Know when, where, and how you’ll vote. Make it a date with democracy!
- **Avoid Fake News Fiascos:** Get your election info from trusted sources. Double-check who’s behind the curtain.
- **See Something, Say Something:** Spot disinformation? Report it to the FBI faster than a superhero changing outfits.
- **Social Media Snoop:** Use platform tools to flag suspicious posts spreading voting falsehoods.
- **Official Info Only:** Use official websites like the U.S. Election Assistance Commission or USA.gov to find your polling place details.
- **Donation Detective:** Before you donate, research political committees on the Federal Election Commission (FEC) website.
- **Say No to Bribes:** Don’t sell your vote, not even for a lifetime supply of chocolate.

**See Something? Report It!**

If you smell something fishy, report it to your local FBI field office or submit an electronic tip. Remember to include juicy details like:

- Names of naughty individuals, election workers, or candidates.
- Screenshots of threats against election workers or polling places.
- Social media posts spreading election lies.
- Details of anyone soliciting illegal campaign contributions.

Now, go forth and vote like the informed citizen you are. Remember, democracy isn’t just a spectator sport!
