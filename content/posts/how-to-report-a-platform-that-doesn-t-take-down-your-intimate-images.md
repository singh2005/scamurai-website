Hey, digital warriors! Imagine this: you discover an intimate photo or video of yourself has been shared online without your permission. You report it, but 48 hours later—crickets. The platform hasn't taken it down or even acknowledged your plea. What now? Fear not, because we’ve got your back with a step-by-step guide to reclaim your digital dignity!

### Step 1: Report to the FTC

First things first, you need to file a complaint at [TakeItDown.ftc.gov](https://TakeItDown.ftc.gov). This is your superhero hotline to the Federal Trade Commission (FTC), the agency that makes sure platforms are playing by the rules outlined in the Take It Down Act.

Here's what you do:

- **Visit**: Go to [TakeItDown.ftc.gov](https://TakeItDown.ftc.gov).
- **Report**: Enter the name of the platform and as much detail as possible about where the image was posted. This is your chance to spill the beans!
- **Share**: You can share as much or as little information about yourself as you feel comfortable with. Remember, you're the one calling the shots.
- **Submit**: Hit that submit button like you're smashing a digital mosquito!

### Step 2: Seek Support

If someone has crossed the line and violated your privacy by sharing these images, remember: you're not alone. At [TakeItDown.ftc.gov](https://TakeItDown.ftc.gov), you'll find resources to help stop the image from spreading and connect with organizations that provide support. Plus, you can report the offender to law enforcement. 

### Pro Tip:

Watch the FTC's video guide on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com) for a visual step-by-step walkthrough. It's like having a digital buddy helping you through the process!

### Key Takeaways:

- **Act Quickly**: Time is of the essence! The faster you report, the better.
- **Use the FTC**: The Federal Trade Commission is your ally. Use their resources to hold platforms accountable.
- **Seek Support**: You're not in this alone—resources and organizations are available to support you.

Remember, you've got the power to take action and protect your online privacy. Keep calm and report on!

For more details, visit the original article at [TakeItDown.ftc.gov](https://TakeItDown.ftc.gov).
